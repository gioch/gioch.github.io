# Portfolio Pivot Plan — Toward Hybrid PM / SWE Roles

Goal: reposition `index.html` so it lands for **Technical PM / Product Engineer / EM-of-small-team / AI Product Lead** roles *and* still works for senior SWE openings.

## What HR / founders actually scan for (in ~10 seconds)

- **PM-leaning roles:** outcomes with numbers, stakeholder management, evidence you translate business → tech → business, roadmap/requirements artifacts. They want *judgment*, not just shipping.
- **Senior SWE / AI eng roles:** specific systems built, scale, AI tooling depth, GitHub.
- **Hybrid (real target):** both, plus a clear story for *why* the dual skillset compounds.

## What's weak right now

1. `index.html:24` — "Full-Stack Software Engineer" pigeonholes. Hybrid roles will skip past.
2. Almost no metrics in Experience (`index.html:122-246`). Co-founder roles especially read as descriptions, not outcomes. "Built X" → "Built X used by N users / cut Y by Z%".
3. The PM / business-translation experience is *invisible* on the page. No mention of requirements gathering, stakeholder syncs, roadmap ownership, or "AI to speed up PM work" — that's the differentiator and it's missing.
4. Highlights (`index.html:44-120`) is 100% backend engineering wins. For a hybrid pivot, 1–2 should be product / leadership / process wins.
5. Tech Stack (`index.html:309-331`) lists no PM tooling (Linear / Jira / Notion / Figma / Miro). Recruiters keyword-filter on these.
6. Competitions (`index.html:249-278`) — 2012 / 2013 / 2015 hackathons feel like padding at 16 years. Keep the best one, drop the rest.
7. Current Interests (`index.html:333-348`) — "Investments / Stock Market / Money Psychology" doesn't reinforce either narrative.

## What to remove / shrink

- Sidekiq optimization deep-dive (`index.html:81-92`) — keep one line, lose the bullets, or move below product wins.
- The two older hackathons.
- "Money Psychology" interest line.

## What to add

- New highlight: **"Product & Delivery Leadership"** — translating business needs into specs, breaking down work for devs, running stakeholder syncs. One sentence + concrete example.
- New highlight: **"AI-Augmented Product Workflows"** — using LLMs to accelerate spec writing, ticket breakdown, status updates, customer-research synthesis. This is the *unique* angle.
- For Shelfy / Resoly / Momntum.ai: add a line each about the PM dimension (decided scope, talked to customers, prioritized roadmap).
- A "Tools" sub-list under Tech Stack: Linear, Jira, Notion, Figma, Miro (whatever is actually used).
- Consider re-titling `index.html:24` to **"Senior Engineer & Technical Product Lead"** or **"Product-Minded Engineer"**.

## Plan, in order

1. **Reposition the headline + About** to dual-track (~15 min).
2. **Rewrite Shelfy / Resoly / Momntum descriptions** to lead with outcomes and surface the PM work (~30 min).
3. **Add 2 product / leadership highlights**, demote one engineering-heavy one (~20 min).
4. **Trim Competitions, swap Interests, add PM tools** (~10 min).
5. **(Optional, high-leverage)** keep `index.html` as the hybrid version and add `index-pm.html` / `index-eng.html` variants to hand out per role. ~1 hour each. Alternative: single resume + tailored cover-letter blurb per application.

## Before starting step 1 / 2

Need 1–2 real metrics per recent role (Shelfy, Resoly, Momntum.ai) — users, revenue, time saved, conversations handled, channels integrated, anything quantifiable. Without numbers, the rewrites won't land.
